Sketch a cabinet in 3D and walk into the shop with a cut diagram five minutes later. Panelizer is built for speed, not to replace full CAD — for parametric modeling, SketchUp or Fusion 360 are the right tools.

## ✨ What it does

- 🛠️ **Model in 3D** — add, move and resize panels with snapping that lines parts up, and see overlaps before you cut.
- 📐 **Your units** — millimetres, centimetres or inches as shop fractions; fields do the math (`23 3/4 + 1/2`).
- 🪵 **Cutlists** — nests your parts onto the sheets you have, respecting grain, kerf and margins. Print it, or export DXF for your CNC.
- 🎨 **Finishes** — wood, stone, metal or paint in any colour, and a **Present** view to show a client the piece as built.
- 🔗 **Share** — send a link that holds the whole design; it opens on a phone too.
- 🔒 **Private** — runs entirely in your browser. No account, no server, no uploads.
