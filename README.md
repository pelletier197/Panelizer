## 💡 Why Panelizer?

Designing shop furniture usually means juggling tools: you model in one app, export to another to get a part list, and hunt down a plugin for your cut layouts. **Panelizer folds all of that into one place.**

> **Model, extract parts, and generate optimized cutlists — together, for free, directly in your browser.**

It’s built for **speed and simplicity**, not to replace heavyweight CAD. 
* **Need full parametric modeling?** SketchUp or Fusion 360 are the right tools.
* **Want to sketch a cabinet and walk into the shop with a cut diagram 5 minutes later?** That’s Panelizer.

## ✨ Features

### 🛠️ Interactive 3D Editing
* **Direct Manipulation:** Add sides, shelves and backs, then move and resize them right in the 3D workspace — or type exact sizes and positions.
* **Smart Snapping:** Assembly-aware snapping lines up edges and butts parts together; snap a corner straight onto another part's corner.
* **Everyday Editing:** Copy, paste, duplicate, quarter-turn, hide and box-select parts, with undo for everything. Press `?` for every shortcut.
* **Measure:** Click two corners to read the distance between them.
* **Rounded Corners:** Give any corner of a panel a radius.
* **Error Prevention:** Visual overlap detection catches mistakes *before* you make a cut.
* **Ultimate Flexibility:** Mix multiple materials and thicknesses in the exact same build.

### 📐 Built for Real Woodworking
Design in millimetres, centimetres or inches. Inches read as shop-friendly fractions down to the precision you work to (1/16", 1/32"…), and every size snaps to that grid — what you see is exactly what you cut. Fields do the arithmetic for you: type `30+15+2-3`, or `23 3/4 + 1/2`. Switching a design between metric and inches converts every part onto the new grid.

### 📋 Automatic Parts List
Every panel becomes a manufacturing part automatically. Get a clean, real-time list detailing **dimensions, material, thickness, and quantity**, and copy it as CSV for a spreadsheet or your supplier.

### 🪵 Smart Cutlist Generation
*One of Panelizer's biggest strengths.* Tell it what sheet goods you have on hand, and its optimization engine handles the rest:
- 🧩 **Nests parts** onto sheets to minimize waste — in saw-friendly straight cuts, or packed freely for a CNC.
- 🌲 **Respects grain direction:** wood grain follows each part's long edge, or set it by hand.
- 🪚 **Accounts for kerf** and edge margins.
- 🚨 **Flags missing stock** or insufficient sheets, and adds the right sheet in one click.
- ✅ **Skips parts you've already cut**, so a half-finished build still adds up.
- 🖨️ **Prints** the cut diagrams, or saves them as a PDF.
- 🤖 **Exports each sheet as a DXF** for your CNC's CAM software, rounded corners as true arcs — one sheet or all of them at once.

*The result? A visual cut diagram that's actually useful at the saw — or the router.*

### 🎨 Finishes & Presentation
Give each material a finish — paint, wood (maple, oak, walnut…), stone (marble, granite…) or metal — and stain it any colour, from palettes of popular furniture finishes or your own. Hit **Present** to see the piece as built: real finishes under soft studio light, with soft shadows. Orbit it, jump between preset angles, and save the view as an image to show your client.

### 🔗 Projects & Sharing
* **Projects:** Every design is saved in your browser as you work. Switch between them from the project name.
* **Files:** Export a design as a `.panelizer.json` file and open it anywhere, or back up every project in one file.
* **Share Links:** Copy a link that holds the whole design. Whoever opens it gets their own copy; opening an updated link later offers to replace that copy rather than add another.

### 📱 On Your Phone
Open a shared link on a phone to look the piece over, measure it, and read the cutlist at the store or in the shop.

### 🔒 100% Local & Private
Everything runs in your browser. **No accounts, no cloud, no server, no uploads, no limit.** Projects stay in your browser's storage, and a share link carries the design inside the link itself — it's never sent to a server. Browsers can clear their storage, so export the projects you care about.
